The season arrives

Her existing words stay exactly as she wrote them. The only change is that the piece is broken into sections with headings, so a reader can find their place and come back to it.

Right now this article and the others run 600 to 900 words with no subheadings at all, which is why they read as a wall on a phone.

A pull quote gives her best line room to breathe, and gives the page a second place for the eye to land.
