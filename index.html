How It Works

We build first. You decide after.

Most web designers ask you to pay up front and imagine the result. We do it backwards: we build your site first, send you the link, and you only move forward if you like what you see.

1

You get a link

We build a complete preview of your site using your real business: your reviews, your photos, your services, your town.

2

You take a look

Like it? A $200 deposit starts the real build. Don't like it? Delete the email and nothing happens. No pressure, no follow-up calls.

3

We finish it together

A 15-minute call to get your details right, then we polish the site, wire up your contact form, and connect your domain.

4

You go live in a week

Balance is due at launch. From day one, customers searching your name find a real website instead of nothing.

About

Built in New Jersey, for New Jersey businesses.

Northlight Web Design is run by David Kincade, based in central New Jersey. Before building websites, David spent years working in landscaping and restaurants, which is exactly why Northlight exists: the best tradespeople and service businesses are often the ones with no website at all.

Their work speaks for itself in person, but online they're invisible, and every week customers who search for them find a competitor instead.

Northlight fixes that with honest pricing and zero runaround. We work mostly with painters, landscapers, and other home service pros across the Jersey Shore and central New Jersey.

If you got an email from us with a preview of your site, that link is real, it was built specifically for your business, and the person who replies when you write back is David.
