Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

The things business owners actually ask us.

Is the preview really free?
Yes. Building the preview is our marketing cost, not yours. If you don't want it, nothing happens - no invoice, no calls, no hard feelings. You only ever pay if you say "build it."
Why is it so much cheaper than other quotes I've gotten?
Agencies carry offices, salespeople, and account managers, and their pricing carries them too. Northlight is deliberately lean and builds for one kind of client - local service businesses - so the work is efficient and the price stays flat.
What if I want changes to the preview?
That's the point of the 15-minute call. We go through the site together, you tell us what's off, and we fix it before launch. Reasonable revisions are part of the flat price.
Do I have to pay monthly?
No. The care plan is optional - it covers hosting, updates, and edits so you never think about the site again. Skip it and the site is still yours; we hand over everything at launch.
How do you know so much about my business already?
Everything in your preview comes from what you've already made public - your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your photos. We just put it to work in one place that you own.
