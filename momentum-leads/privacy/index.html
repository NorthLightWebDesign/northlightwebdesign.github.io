Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026

Momentum Leads ("we," "us," or "our") helps people in New Jersey find affordable final expense and life insurance coverage by connecting them with licensed insurance agents. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or submit a form, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit a form or request a quote, we collect the information you give us, which may include your name, phone number, email address, age or date of birth, ZIP code or town, and whether you are a veteran. We may also collect basic technical information such as your device type and the ad or page you came from.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about final expense and life insurance coverage.
  • To connect you with one or more licensed insurance agents who can prepare a quote for you.
  • To respond to your questions and improve our advertising and service.

How we share your information

We share the information you submit with licensed insurance agents and the agencies they work with, so they can contact you and provide a quote. We may use service providers (for example, form, hosting, and consent-verification services) that process information on our behalf. We do not sell your information to unrelated third parties, and we do not share it for their own independent marketing.

Consent to be contacted. By submitting a form, you agree that Momentum Leads and the licensed insurance agents we work with may contact you at the phone number and email you provide, including by phone call, text message, and automated or prerecorded means, about insurance products and services. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time as described below.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying STOP to a text, telling an agent on a call, or emailing us at the address below.
  • You can ask us to access or delete the information we hold about you.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Advertising

We advertise on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram and may use their tools to measure how our ads perform. These platforms have their own privacy policies that govern their use of data.

Contact us
